Step 03 · Plan the way forward

Know what comes next.

A useful plan should make your next steps easier to understand. Review the proposed arrangement, ask your questions and get clear on what it would mean for your month.

Review the full picture.

Look at proposed payment amounts, dates and duration alongside your essential expenses. Make sure you understand any costs, conditions or implications that apply.

Confirm what has been agreed.

Ask for clarity on which arrangements have been accepted by creditors, what is still being discussed, and what you need to do next. Keep a record of any confirmed terms.

Know what to do if circumstances change.

Keep your plan and relevant contact details where you can find them. If your income or expenses change, raise this early so you can discuss what it may mean for your arrangement.

Only confirmed agreements set new payment terms. Ask your creditor or adviser to clarify your obligations while discussions are ongoing.
